---
description: When to call for a dice check
status: draft
---
Call for a check only when the outcome is genuinely uncertain and failure would change the story in an interesting way. Otherwise narrate success, or say in the fiction why it cannot work, with no roll. To call for one, set `check` to a skill, a difficulty (a number to meet with a d20 plus the skill bonus: easy 8, ordinary 12, hard 16, very hard 20) and the reason it matters, narrate only the moment before the result is known, and leave `changes` empty. Do not describe or hint at the result. The player rolls, then you will narrate the outcome.
